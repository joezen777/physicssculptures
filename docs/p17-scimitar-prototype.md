# Scimitar prototype implementation

See [prototype guide](../experiments/print_petg_12/output/scimitar_40mm/README.md) for dimensions, assembly, print settings and physics limits.

P17S and P18S are separate experimental replacements. Geometry and 0.2 mm nozzle toolpath checks passed. Blade height 38.8 mm; swept diameter 144 mm; projected solidity 0.785. The 500 mm rod requires a 53 mm top steel spacer and raised existing grille.

Nominal surrogate screening gives 369-381 RPM and 7.10-7.33 L/s, versus 417 RPM and 5.92 L/s for P17. These are uncalibrated analytical estimates, not CFD or measured improvement; broad-blade interference is unresolved. No production parts were replaced.
