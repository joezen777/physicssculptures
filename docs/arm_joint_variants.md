# Overlapping Krull arm joints

See [print and assembly instructions](../experiments/print_petg_12/output/arm_joint_variants/README.md).

Temporary T15 retains the original printed short arm and uses a split cradle/cap to distribute bending and torsional reactions along its outer segment. The single old cup-hole screw retains the split joint; it does not provide a guarantee of uniform contact or eliminate the original root load.

Permanent S14/S15 uses an approximately 40 mm rectangular socket with top/bottom/side bearing faces, two horizontal M4 bolts 16 mm apart, and 24 mm steel compression sleeves. Sleeves bypass PETG for axial bolt preload; tongue/socket faces carry distributed bearing loads after clearance is taken up. This trades a larger hub and extra steel hardware for side service access. It remains a prototype without fatigue or creep qualification.

Preserve both the Krull-inspired sweep and broad overlapping bearing surfaces in future changes. Avoid relying on adhesive to compensate for excessive clearance. P04 requires a separate diagnosis; see the release guide and bearing-spacer-contact.md.
