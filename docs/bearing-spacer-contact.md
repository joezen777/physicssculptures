# Bearing spacer contact and motion

See ../experiments/print_petg_12/output/bearing_spacers_motion.png and the editable SVG beside it. The illustration is a schematic section: bearing internal proportions, shields and rolling-element geometry are not manufacturer dimensions. It shows the intended contact and motion from PRINT_AND_ASSEMBLE.md.

Spacers contact stationary bearing inner rings only; the outer rings and attached printed housings rotate. Fixed planet carriers give ring-to-sun ratio -2 and ring-to-planet spin ratio +4 (72/36/18 teeth). Planet centers do not orbit. Each sun drives the next rotor through its hex connector.

The previous conversational acceptance of 12 mm OD / 10 mm ID aluminum tubing was too broad. With an M8 shaft it can shift approximately 1 mm sideways from centered; at that offset its outer edge reaches radius 7 mm about the bearing axis. Actual bearing inner-ring face and shield clearance must be checked before accepting it. Neither the original 12 mm OD nor this alternative is validated against a specific purchased bearing face geometry by this schematic. Do not force a rod through an undersized tube or use it as a cutting tool.
