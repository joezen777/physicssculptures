# Zoetrope engraving on P02 and P03

The saved pot_zoetrope UV-strip SVGs are cut into the current PETG prototype parts. P02 uses ball-uv-strip-ccw.svg and P03 uses ball-uv-strip-cw.svg, matching the +1, -2, +4 stack when the bottom input turns counterclockwise viewed from above. Both P02 instances share the same artwork.

The original 24 frames, five platforms per frame, one ball per frame and all 144 closed contours are retained. The source SVGs remain unchanged. The old developed cone stencil targeted a 120/168 mm diameter, 58 mm tall pot, so it is not used directly on the newer 120/176 mm diameter, 80 mm tall shell. Instead, the original UV page is mapped with theta = 2*pi*x/452.389342 and z = 11 + 58 - y. A centered 58 mm artwork band leaves the upper and lower mechanical features clear.

The black shapes become inward recesses, 0.6 mm deep in the radial direction. The original 3.6 mm radial wall retains at least 3.0 mm beneath every recess. Angular wedges intersect a conical depth-limiting shell so the recess follows the taper. Platform corner mapping and 32-vertex CAD ball outlines approximate the saved paths within about 0.01 mm at this taper. The 96-vertex source balls are reduced to 32 vertices for the CAD cuts to keep the models practical to open and slice. No alignment outline, frame separator, border or background is cut.

The original platform dimensions are 4 by 1 mm and balls are 1.6 mm diameter in UV coordinates. Horizontal surface size varies with the new taper. The 1.2 mm nozzle kit retains its size and slicer settings; these fine marks may be softened at that nozzle size. Inspect the actual print for contrast and detail. Darkened recesses in the detail preview illustrate the cut surfaces, not an added material or automatic multicolor print.

The original optical caveats still apply: continuous rotation alone does not establish the apparent bounce, and differently geared levels do not share one-frame-per-sample timing. See pot-zoetrope-emboss.md for the animation and sampling notes. This change manufactures the pattern; it does not add a strobe or optical viewing apparatus. Physical optical performance remains untested.

etch_zoetrope.py reads the original SVG files and caches valid shell geometry outside the repository using source hashes. It verifies that every recess intersects the wall, every contour center is empty at mid-depth, total removed volume matches the sum of the 144 expected cuts, the complete inner liner remains intact, and the result is one valid solid. update_etched_pots.py applies the validated cuts to the existing part solids and replaces only P02_pot_fan_A_print-2x.stl and P03_pot_fan_B_print-1x.stl in the loose STL folder. It checks closed single-component meshes, unchanged external envelopes and the expected removed volume. Only these two parts are sliced. The complete CAD assembly, exploded views, other loose STLs, archives and hardware sprues are unchanged. The erroneous resin_sprue_sheets output folder was deleted at the user's request. A separate two-part CAD view and previews live with the zoetrope experiment for inspection.
